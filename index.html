The work · 06

Six apps. Each one live.

Every card opens the real thing. Small enough to understand at a glance, sharp enough to use every day.

01TV tracker

SimklApp

Every show you follow on one dashboard. Gemini adds recommendations and a latest-news search, using your own free API key, which stays in your browser.

simkl.michaelsavastano.com
02Time-travel explorer

ChronoVisor

Pick any place on the globe and any moment in history, or the future, and Gemini renders what you would see there. Wrapped in a cyan starship-console HUD.

chronovisor.michaelsavastano.com
03Typing tutor

AI Typing

Learn to type with an AI tutor: guided lessons, real-time feedback on the keys you miss, and focused practice. Calm, scholarly, and built to remove distraction.

aityping.michaelsavastano.com
04Crossword builder

Across

Build crosswords on a grid that reads like the newspaper puzzle page: ink on newsprint, square corners, and the black squares doing the talking.

buildacross.vercel.app
05Fiction library

StoryVault

A private reading room for short fiction. One quiet, closed-door shelf for the stories worth keeping.

storyvault.michaelsavastano.com
06Memory agent

Skill Memory

A chat assistant that remembers you. After every reply it edits its own memory, then pulls back the notes that matter next time. Runs on Gemini or Claude with your own key.

feedback.michaelsavastano.com

01

One job each

Every app starts from a single task, like tracking shows or building a crossword, and leaves out anything that doesn't serve it.

02

A look that fits the subject

Type, color and texture come from what the app is about, so a time machine feels like a starship and a crossword feels like the puzzle page.

03

AI where it earns its place

The AI recommends, tutors, renders and remembers where it makes the app better, never as a gimmick bolted on the side.
